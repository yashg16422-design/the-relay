The stand-in's settings were fitted on the real calls saved up to 2026-11-12 (146 understand, 144 judge, 19 draft, 10 repair); the Real columns include the calls made since. Draft and repair rest on few real calls, so their p95 is close to the largest value seen.

Time to first chunk is fitted (real draft p50 489.3 ms, n=105) but cannot be checked in-process: the in-process transport hands over a streamed reply all at once. The user-visible first token comes after the sentence gate, so it is unaffected.

## Where the stand-in is least like Groq

1. **Slowdown under load is one weak number.** `load_delay_per_inflight_ms` comes from a single probe of 20 tiny simultaneous calls (E4). Real latency under a burst of full-size prompts is not measured.
2. **Latency does not depend on prompt or reply length for the non-streamed steps.** Each call draws from one lognormal per step, fitted on three pooled models.
3. **Tokens are counted at 4 bytes each and billed output is resampled** from real counts, most of which were measured under the old 100- and 120-token caps.
4. **Draft and repair fits rest on 19 and 10 real calls**, so their p95 is one observation.
5. **Tokens per day comes from Groq's documentation**, not from a limit this account has hit (requests per minute was confirmed by probe E2). The stand-in's 429 body is its own wording, not the captured one.
